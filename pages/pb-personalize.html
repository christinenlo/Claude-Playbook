Personalize Cowork

A few optional tweaks that make Claude fit how you work — set your instructions, turn on memory, and confirm your connectors.

Settings

Open Settings from your name (bottom-left). It's split into a few groups — here's what each one is for.

SettingWhat it's for
GeneralThe basics: appearance and motion (turn down on-screen animation), Voice (the language, style, and speed Claude uses when you talk to it out loud), and Notifications — get pinged when a response or a long task finishes, so you don't have to sit and watch it run.
AccountYour profile and sign-in. Includes trusted devices and active sessions — every place you're currently signed in. If you spot one you don't recognize, you can sign it out here.
UsageHow much of your limit you've used and when it resets — full details in Token usage.
CapabilitiesSwitch Claude features on or off — most importantly Memory (what Claude remembers about you from chat to chat). See Memory.
CoworkSettings that apply only to Cowork mode.
Desktop app → GeneralPreferences for the desktop app itself — things like how it launches and behaves on your computer.
Desktop app → ExtensionsLet Claude use apps, data, and tools on your computer. This is for desktop use — not the same as connectors (which are for the web).
SkillsSee and manage the skills you can run with / — see the Org Skills Directory.
ConnectorsLink Claude to your web tools (Microsoft 365, SharePoint, and so on). The Claude Chrome extension is a connector too — it's what lets Claude work in your browser. See Connectors.
Extensions vs. connectorsExtensions let Claude work with apps and files on your computer (desktop). Connectors link Claude to web-based tools. The one that trips people up: the Claude Chrome extension is managed as a connector — it's what powers Claude in Chrome.

Instructions for Claude optional

Instructions are account-wide settings that tell Claude how you generally like it to work — so you don't have to repeat yourself in every chat. Claude keeps them in mind across all of your conversations.

To set your instructions:

  1. Click your initials in the lower-left corner.
  2. Select Settings.
  3. Under Instructions for Claude, describe how you'd like Claude to work — such as:
    • Your preferred approaches or methods
    • Common terms or concepts you use
    • Typical scenarios you encounter
    • General communication preferences
Applies everywhereAnything you add here is applied to all of your conversations with Claude — across both Chat and Cowork.

Some examples you could add

  • “Keep answers concise and skimmable — use bullets over long paragraphs, and lead with the answer.”
  • “I work in HOA/condo insurance — use our terms (SOV, ACORD, COI, master policy) without over-explaining the basics.”
  • “Before filling any carrier form or portal, show me a summary of the values you'll enter and wait for my go-ahead.”
  • “Only search the web when I explicitly ask you to.”
  • “Use a professional, friendly tone for anything board- or client-facing.”

When you're done, click Save changes to apply them (or Discard to cancel).

Totally optionalOnly set these if you want Claude to stay consistent across every chat and Cowork session. You can edit or clear them anytime.

Memory optional

Optional, but handy. With memory on, Claude remembers context from your past chats — your role, the accounts and carriers you work with, and how you like things done — so you don't have to re-explain yourself every time.

Why it helps: Claude picks up where you left off and gives more relevant, consistent answers across chats. For example, it can remember that you work in HOA insurance, which skills you run most often, and your formatting preferences — so you spend less time setting up context and more time getting work done.

Turn it on

  1. Your name (bottom-left) → Settings → Capabilities.
  2. Toggle on Generate memory from chat history.
Good to knowView or edit what Claude remembers anytime in Capabilities → View and manage memory. Learn more.

View and manage memory

Curious what Claude has picked up about you? In Capabilities, click View and manage memory to open a summary of everything Claude remembers — your work context, what's been top of mind lately, and a brief history. Claude refreshes this automatically each night.

To change it, press the pencil and tell Claude what to add or correct — for example, “I mostly handle condo (HOA) renewals” or “always show me the SOV values before filling a form.” Then send it.

Keep it work-appropriateDon't add sensitive personal details — no passwords, birthdays, financial or health information, or anything you wouldn't want Claude using across every chat.

Connectors

Connectors let Claude work with your tools. Make sure the three below are connected so Cowork and skills work properly.

Check your connectors

  1. Click your name (bottom-left) → Settings → Connectors.
  2. Confirm these three show a checkmark under Status:
    • Microsoft 365
    • Microsoft Learn
    • Claude in Chrome
  3. If any isn't connected, click Connect next to it and follow the prompts.
Microsoft connectors are read-only by defaultOut of the box, Claude can read from SharePoint, Teams, and Outlook but can’t change anything (no sending, editing, or deleting). An admin can optionally turn on write actions (like sending email or creating files); Teams always stays read-only.

Try a quick test

Not sure a connector is working? Ask Claude to pull something small — if it comes back with the right info, you're connected. Try:

Summarize my 3 most recent Outlook emails.Checks the Outlook (Microsoft 365) connection.
Find the SOV for Sample Ridge HOA in our SharePoint and show me the total insured value.Checks SharePoint access.
What was posted in our renewals Teams channel this week?Checks the Teams connection.
