Scheduled

A scheduled task runs on its own on a repeating schedule you set — so routine work happens automatically without you starting it each time.

What it's good for

  • A morning summary of your new emails or the day's renewals.
  • A weekly recap of what's still outstanding.
  • Any routine check you'd otherwise have to remember to run yourself.

Setting one up

Open Scheduled tasks from the left sidebar to see your tasks, then click New task (top-right).

You get two ways to make one:

Create with Claude — just describe what you want; Claude explains, asks a few quick questions, and sets it up for you.

Set up manually — fill in a Name, a Description, the instructions (what Claude should do), optionally a project to work in, and the Frequency (Manual, Hourly, Daily, Weekdays, or Weekly), then Save.

Quick shortcut: type /schedule in any existing chat or task to turn it into a scheduled one — or just ask Claude in plain English, “set up a daily summary of my new emails at 9 am.”

When it runs

At the scheduled time you get a notification, and the run shows up under Scheduled in the sidebar — open it to see what Claude did.

Managing your tasks

Open any scheduled task to Run now, Pause or resume it, Edit it, review its past runs, or Delete it.

Write it like you won't be thereA scheduled task runs on its own, so be specific in the instructions — for example, “summarize only today's new emails” — so it does the right thing without you around to clarify.
Keep in mindA scheduled task only runs while Claude is open and your computer is awake. If your computer is asleep at the scheduled time, that run is skipped — Claude catches it up once when you're back. To stop your computer sleeping through a run, turn on Keep computer awake in Settings → Desktop app → General.
