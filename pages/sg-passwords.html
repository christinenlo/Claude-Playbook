Passwords & Bookmarks

Never save personal passwords in your Claude profile.Copy one over only when you need it, and don’t let Chrome save it.

🔐 Turn off autofill & password saving Optional

Recommended, so the profile can’t auto-save or auto-fill logins. In your Claude profile, open Chrome menu → Passwords and autofill → Google Password Manager → Settings, and turn off:

  • Offer to save passwords and passkeys
  • Automatically create a passkey to sign in faster
  • Sign in automatically

Passwords

  1. In your personal profile, click the three dots (top-right)… → Passwords and autofill → Google Password Manager.
  2. Find the site (e.g. PMA, Arden, Epic)… → open it and copy the password.
  3. Paste it into the login in your Claude profile.
  4. If Chrome offers to save it, click Never.

Bookmarks

  1. Copy the site’s link from your personal profile…
  2. …open it in your Claude profile and bookmark it with the star.
