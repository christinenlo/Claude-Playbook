Passwords & Bookmarks

Do NOT save passwords in your new Chrome profile.Keep the secure profile free of saved credentials. Only copy a password over when you actually need it.

Passwords

  1. In your personal profile, click the three dots (top-right)… → Passwords and autofill → Google Password Manager.
  2. Find the site you need (e.g. PMA, Arden, Epic)… → open it and copy the password.
  3. Paste it into the login on your new profile.
  4. If prompted to save it, click Never.

Bookmarks

  1. Copy the link from your personal profile…
  2. …open it in the new profile and bookmark it with the star icon.
