Passwords & Bookmarks

🔐 Turn off autofill & password saving Optional

💡 Recommended, but optional.Turning off Chrome’s password saving & autofill keeps this profile from auto-saving or auto-filling your credentials. Either way, never save personal passwords in this profile.

Open Password Manager → Settings (Chrome menu → Passwords and autofill → Google Password Manager → Settings) and turn off these three toggles:

  • Offer to save passwords and passkeys
  • Automatically create a passkey to sign in faster
  • Sign in automatically
Never save personal passwords in your new Chrome profile.Keep it free of your personal credentials — only copy one over when you actually need it, and don’t let Chrome save it.

Passwords

  1. In your personal profile, click the three dots (top-right)… → Passwords and autofill → Google Password Manager.
  2. Find the site you need (e.g. PMA, Arden, Epic)… → open it and copy the password.
  3. Paste it into the login on your new profile.
  4. If prompted to save it, click Never.

Bookmarks

  1. Copy the link from your personal profile…
  2. …open it in the new profile and bookmark it with the star icon.
