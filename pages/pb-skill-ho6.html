HO-6 Flyer

/lo-ho-6-generator — a personal-lines recommendation flyer for condo & townhome unit owners.

📝 Full guide coming soonA detailed step-by-step walkthrough is on the way. Here’s the quick version in the meantime.

What it does

Builds a one-page LaBarre Oksnee HO-6 flyer (Word .docx) that tells a condo or townhome unit owner what personal insurance to carry — worded to match the association’s master policy. It only lists the coverages and deductibles the master policy actually carries, and adapts the unit-owner section to the master property form (bare-walls, single-entity, or all-in).

How to run it

  1. Attach the association’s master policy proposal into Claude (the SB1525 disclosure, dec pages, or a coverage summary work too).
  2. Run /lo-ho-6-generator.
  3. Claude reads the master policy and builds the recommended-coverage breakdown from it, then produces the finished flyer on LaBarre Oksnee letterhead — ready to hand to owners or the management company.
