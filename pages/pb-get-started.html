Get Started with Cowork

Cowork is Claude with hands. Chat only talks back; Cowork actually does the work — opens your files, fills carrier portals, builds documents and runs our skills — while you do something else. Describe the task in plain English and it checks in when it needs you.

Chat vs. Cowork

ModeUse it for
ChatQuestions, drafting, analysis, brainstorming.
Cowork recommendedReal work: carrier forms (Arden, LiO, PMA), ACORDs, comparison sheets, training docs, and skills.
CodeA developer tool — don’t use.

Switch between Chat and Cowork with the toggle in the chat box.

Which model to use

Recommended: Sonnet for everyday tasks

ModelUse it for
Sonnet use thisAlmost everything: ACORDs, comparison sheets, carrier submissions, training docs, board letters.
OpusGenuinely hard work: ambiguous policy language, conflicting SOVs, building skills.
HaikuQuick, simple jobs: triaging emails, pulling data from a COI, short replies.
Fable avoidSkip it — much slower than Opus and uses far more of your limit, with no benefit for our work.

Effort and Thinking

Click the model name in the chat box to switch models. The same menu has Effort — how hard Claude works. More effort is more thorough, but slower and uses your limits faster.

  • High (the default) — everyday work.
  • Low / Medium — quick, simple asks.
  • Extra / Max — only for genuinely hard problems. Max uses 2.5× or more of your usage, so don’t leave it on.

Thinking lets Claude reason longer on complex tasks. Keep it off for routine work.

Try it — a few starter prompts

On Cowork and Sonnet, paste one of these:

What's the weather in Los Angeles this week? The simplest test — watch it search and answer.
Summarize this document and list the key dates and dollar amounts. Attach a PDF, Word or Excel file with the + button first.
Draft a short, friendly renewal reminder email to a board. Always edit before you send.
The golden ruleCowork does the heavy lifting, but you’re the expert. Review everything before it goes to a carrier or client.
