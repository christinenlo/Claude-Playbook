🚀 Get Started with Cowork

What is Cowork? Think of Cowork as Claude with hands. Regular chat just talks back — Cowork can actually do the work: open your files, fill out carrier portals in the browser, build documents, and run our custom skills, all while you watch or work on something else. You give it a task in plain English and it gets it done, checking in when it needs you.

Chat vs. Cowork

InterfaceWhat it isUse it for
ChatStandard Claude conversation (same as in the browser)Questions, drafting text, analysis, feedback, brainstorming.
Cowork recommendedClaude wired into your files & tools to actually do the workFilling insurance forms (Arden, LIO, PMA), building ACORDs, comparison sheets, training docs, and running skills.
CodeCommand-line tool for developersDo not use.
How to tell them apartIn the top-left of the app you'll see two icons: 💬 Chat (a speech bubble) and ☰ Cowork (the little stacked-lines / tasks icon). Make sure Cowork is selected for real work.

Switch between Chat and Cowork with the toggle inside the chat box.

Which model to use

Recommended: Sonnet for everyday tasks

Sonnet handles almost everything — reach for Opus only on the genuinely hard stuff. Skip Fable: it’s much slower than Opus and uses far more of your limit, with no upside for insurance work.

ModelBest forExamples
Fable avoidNothing day-to-day — skip it for insurance work.It’s much slower than Opus and burns through your usage far faster, with no upside for our tasks — use Sonnet or Opus instead.
OpusComplex reasoning & agentic codingReviewing a master policy for ambiguous exclusions; reconciling conflicting SOVs; modeling loss-run trends; drafting nuanced coverage explanations for a contentious board dispute.
Sonnet use thisBest balance of speed & intelligenceACORD applications from an SOV; comparison sheets; LIO/Arden/PMA submissions; training docs; board letters; analyzing an SOV for coverage gaps.

Try it — a few starter prompts

New to Cowork? Paste one of these into the chat box (make sure you're on the Cowork toggle, Sonnet, Thinking off) to get a feel for it:

Search the web

What's the weather in Los Angeles this week? The simplest test — watch Cowork search and answer.

Pull from a document

Summarize this document and list the key dates and dollar amounts. Attach any PDF, Word, or Excel file with the + button.

Write something

Draft a short, friendly renewal reminder email to a board. Cowork as a writing partner — always edit before you send.
The golden ruleCowork does the heavy lifting, but you're still the expert. Always review what it produces — especially anything going to a carrier or a client — before it goes out.
