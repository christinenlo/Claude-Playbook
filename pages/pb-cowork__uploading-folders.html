Uploading folders

You can give Claude a whole folder to work in: click + and add a folder, or drag one in. Claude can read everything inside and save its work there for the rest of the task.

Add as many folders as you need — e.g. one with the account’s documents, one with templates, and one for the finished files.

Claude only sees what you shareIt has no access to the rest of your computer. If you ask it to save to Downloads but haven’t shared Downloads, it will fail — share the folder first.

Mind the size. Big folders make Claude slower and use your usage faster. Share just what the task needs.
