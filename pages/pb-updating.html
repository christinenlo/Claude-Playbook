Updating Claude

Get in the habit of updating Claude before you start a task — it only takes a few seconds, and keeps you on the latest, most accurate version.

Always stay on the latest version.Whenever a Relaunch to update button appears, click it right away. Newer versions bring better results and accuracy, and keep the guide's screens matching what you see.

The Relaunch to update button shows up at the bottom-left when an update is ready. Click it, then give Claude a few minutes to restart on its own — the window relaunches and the button disappears once you're on the current version.
