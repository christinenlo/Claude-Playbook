How to navigate this guide

  • Menu (left): the main sections. Click one to open it; arrows expand sub-topics. On a phone, tap ☰.
  • Search (top) looks through the whole guide — try a carrier name or “Sonnet.”
  • − / + and the moon (top-right) change the text size and switch light / dark mode. Both are remembered.
  • Previous / Next at the bottom of each page walks you through in order.
