🧭 How to navigate this guide

  • Menu (left): the sidebar lists the main sections — Setup Guide, the Playbook, Skills, Workshops, and Help. Click one to open it; a few expand into sub-topics with a ▾ arrow. On a phone, tap the ☰ button.
  • Search: the box at the top searches the whole guide at once — try a carrier name, “cookies,” or “Sonnet.”
  • Previous / Next: the buttons at the bottom of every page walk you through in order.
  • Updates: the list just below shows what’s changed recently, newest first.
