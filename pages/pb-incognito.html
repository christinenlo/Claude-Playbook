Incognito mode

Temporary chats that aren’t saved to your history or Claude’s memory.

What it is

An incognito chat is a one-off conversation that Claude doesn’t save — it stays out of your chat history, isn’t added to memory, and isn’t used to train the model. Handy for a quick question or a throwaway test you don’t want cluttering things up.

Starting & leaving one

When you start a new chat, click the ghost icon in the top-right corner (or press Ctrl+Shift+I). You’ll get a “You’re incognito” screen with a dark border around the window. To leave, click the X in the top bar (or Ctrl+Shift+I again).

Nothing is savedClose an incognito chat and it’s gone for good — there’s no history to reopen. Don’t use it for account work you’ll need again.
Not hidden from the companyIncognito keeps a chat out of your history and memory, but your admin can still see it. It’s not a way to hide work from the org.

Good to know

  • Incognito only works for standalone chats — not inside a project.
  • You can’t turn an incognito chat into a saved one afterward.
  • Incognito chats don’t show up when you search your chats.
  • Claude still uses your custom style and preferences during the chat.
