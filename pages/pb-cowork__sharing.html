Sharing chats & tasks

You can share a chat, but not a Cowork task — tasks stay yours only.

  1. Open the chat and click Share (top-right).
  2. Choose Shared (anyone at Labarre/Oksnee can view) → Create share link.
  3. Send the link.
What gets sharedA snapshot of the messages up to the moment you press Share — nothing after. Attached files are never shared.

To unshare, open Share again and switch back to Keep private. The old link stops working.
