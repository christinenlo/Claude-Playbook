Artifacts

An artifact is a separate panel where Claude puts something substantial you'll want to keep, reuse, or edit — instead of burying it in the chat. It opens on the right side, next to your conversation.

What can be an artifact

  • Documents — a board letter, a summary, a memo.
  • Tables — a coverage comparison, a list of accounts.
  • Diagrams & flowcharts — a simple process or workflow.
  • One-page pages or forms — a simple handout or intake form.

Web vs. desktop artifacts

Artifacts work both in your web browser (claude.ai) and in the desktop app. Everyday ones — documents, tables, comparisons, simple interactive pages — work in either place; the difference is the live, auto-updating ones.

For our work, use the desktop app — that’s where the live, connected artifacts (like the Email Tracker) run. Plain documents and tables work either place.

Creating one

The easy way — just ask. In any chat, describe what you want; Claude opens an artifact on its own when the content is big enough to stand alone. For example:

Make a one-page comparison table of these two renewal options.
Draft a short board letter explaining the premium increase.

Or start from the Artifacts page. Click Artifacts in the left sidebar to see everything you've made, then click New artifact (top-right).

Choose Create Cowork artifact (or Create chat artifact). Claude then asks a few quick questions about what you want and builds it for you.

Editing it

Don't start over — just tell Claude what to change (“add a column for the deductible,” “make the tone warmer”) and the artifact updates in place. Claude updates the same artifact rather than making a new one each time, and keeps each version — so you can flip back with the version selector, or compare. For documents you can also highlight text and edit it directly.

Saving & sharing

In the artifact's lower-right corner you can Copy it, Download it to your computer, or Publish it to share with others.

It stays with your chat

An artifact is saved inside the conversation it was made in — click its card in the chat to reopen the panel anytime. And if Claude answers in the chat but you'd rather have a clean document, just say “put that in an artifact.”

Always review itAn artifact is a great starting point, but you're still the expert — check anything headed to a carrier or client before it goes out.
