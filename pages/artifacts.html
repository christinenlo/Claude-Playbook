Artifacts

An artifact is something Claude makes for you to keep, edit and share — a document, table, deck or tracker — instead of leaving it buried in the chat. It opens in a panel next to the conversation.

What can be an artifact

  • Docs — a document your team reads, comments on and edits, while Claude keeps it current.
  • Slides — a slide deck.
  • Design — mockups and visuals, made with Claude Design.
  • Tables, trackers & dashboards — a coverage comparison, an account list, or a tracker built on your connected tools.
  • Pages & forms — a one-page handout or intake form.

Docs, Slides and Design are still in beta.

Where artifacts live

They’re saved to your Claude account, so they open anywhere you sign in and can be shared with people at Labarre/Oksnee. When an artifact pulls from Outlook or other connected tools, each viewer sees their own data, not yours.

Older “live artifacts”Ones made before August 19, 2026 — like early Email Tracker dashboards — still work but can’t be edited. To change one, ask Claude to make it again.

Creating one

Just ask. Describe what you want, or start with “I want to make an artifact that…” For example:

Make a one-page comparison table of these two renewal options.
Draft a short board letter explaining the premium increase.

Or use the Artifacts page: click Artifacts in the sidebar, pick Docs, Slides or Design under Make something new, then Start with a prompt.

Find one later with the All / Pinned / Yours / Shared with you tabs, search, or the All types filter.

Editing it

Tell Claude what to change (“add a column for the deductible”) and it updates in place. Every change saves a new version you can go back to. In documents you can also edit text directly, and in a Doc you can @-mention Claude in a comment to make a change.

Sharing & comments

Chat, comments and Share are at the top-right of the artifact.

Click Share to invite people (or a project) with a role, set who has access, and Copy link. Access can be Only people invited or Anyone at Labarre/Oksnee. Anyone with the link is turned off for us, so nobody outside the company can open it.

RoleCan…
OwnerEdit it and manage who has access.
EditorChange it, and give others any role up to Editor.
CommenterSee live updates and comment.
ViewerOpen it and see live updates.

Docs only have view and edit for now.

Comments: click the comments button, then click anywhere on the artifact. You can ask Claude to read the comments and reply.

Client info? Keep it to invited people“Anyone at Labarre/Oksnee” lets anyone in the company with the link open it. For client details, use Only people invited.

It stays with your chat

Click the artifact’s card in its chat to reopen it, or find it on the Artifacts page. If Claude answered in the chat and you want a clean document, say “put that in an artifact.” As always, review it before it goes to a carrier or client.
