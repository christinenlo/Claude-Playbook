1Set Up a Chrome Profile

Chrome only — not Edge.Create a dedicated, separate Chrome profile so your secure work is isolated from your everyday browsing.

Create a Google Chrome profile

  1. In Chrome, click your Profile icon (top-right) → + Add.
  2. When the pop-up appears, click Stay signed out (don't sign the profile into a Google account).
  3. A new window opens. Give it a clear name like Claude Profile and pick a distinct color/theme from your main profile, then click Done.
  4. Your new profile now appears under Other Chrome profiles.
Why the different color?It's a visual cue that you're in your secure zone. Since the profiles are separate, a problem in your everyday profile can't reach your carrier-portal session in the secure one.
