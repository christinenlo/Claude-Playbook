3Set Up Claude Desktop (Windows)

Claude Desktop is already installed — open it, pin it, and log in.

  1. Type “Claude” into the Windows search bar and open the app.
  2. Right-click Claude in the taskbar → Pin to taskbar.
  3. Click Get Started, log in with your email, and click Next through setup. Then set the toggle in the chat box to Cowork.
  4. Click the model name in the chat box and pick Sonnet. Leave Effort on High (the default).

Error on startup? See Troubleshooting.
