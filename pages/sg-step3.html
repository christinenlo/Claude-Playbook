3Set Up Claude Desktop (Windows)

Claude Desktop is already installed on your computer — you just need to open it, pin it, and log in.

  1. Open Claude. Type “Claude” into the Windows search bar (bottom-left), then click the Claude app (or Open) to launch it.
  2. Once it’s open, Claude appears in your taskbar. Right-click it → Pin to taskbar.
  3. Open Claude → Get Started → log in with your email → click Next through setup. On the main screen, make sure you're on the Cowork tab (the Chat / Cowork toggle sits inside the chat box).
  4. Set your model: click the model name (bottom-right of the chat) and choose Sonnet for everyday work. Next to it you’ll see an effort level (Low / Medium / High) — Medium is fine.

Seeing an error on startup? See Troubleshooting for the common messages and what to do.
