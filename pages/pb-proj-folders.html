Folders & sharing

Adding a folder

In the Folder panel, click + and pick a folder on your computer. Claude can read its files and save work back there. (Or use Use a folder when creating the project.)

  • It’s Only you — teammates don’t see it; they add their own.
  • Claude can only reach it while Claude Desktop is open (see Claude in the cloud).
  • For files the whole team should use, upload them to Context instead.

Sharing a project

Company-wide projects are turned off for us, so every project is Private. To share one: open it → Share → invite people and choose view or edit. They’ll find it under Shared with you.

They get the project’s instructions and context, but not your chats — everyone sees only their own. To show someone a conversation, share that chat.
