Along the top

The pin keeps it in your sidebar (see Pin your projects), Share invites people, and ⋮ has more options.

The chat box

Anything you start here is saved to the project. Below it, Chats and tasks lists your work and Activity shows what’s been happening. Your chats stay private to you unless you share one.
