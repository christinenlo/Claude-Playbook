Pin your projects

Keep the projects you use most one click away.

Three ways to pin (or unpin):

  • Inside a project — the pin icon at the top-right.
  • All Projects page — a card’s ⋮ menu → Pin.
  • Sidebar — hover a project → ⋮ → Pin.

Pinned projects sit at the top of the sidebar. To see them all, click the ↗ arrow next to “Projects.”

Click a pinned project to show or hide its recent chats right in the sidebar.
