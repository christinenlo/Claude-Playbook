How to prompt Claude

No special wording needed — just be clear. Three things matter most:

  1. Be specific — what you want, for which account, in what format.
  2. Give an example — a sample, a past file, or the format you’re after.
  3. Correct, don’t restart — tell it what to change: “the year built should be 1998, not 1988.”
Instead ofTry
“Fill out the Arden form.”“Run /lo-arden-submission for Sample Ridge HOA using the attached SOV. Show me the values before submitting.”
“Write a letter to the board.”“Draft a board letter explaining the 12% premium increase on the Sample Oaks renewal — under 200 words, professional but warm.”
“Make a comparison sheet.”“Build a comparison sheet for Sample Villas: expiring vs. the two attached renewal options. Highlight the deductible and wind/hail differences.”
Have Claude confirm before it startsFor a submission or a bulk change, say “summarize what you’re going to do, then wait for my OK.” If the plan is off, you catch it before any work happens.

Prefer talking? Click the microphone in the chat box, or press Ctrl+D, and dictate. Voice settings are under Settings → General → Voice.
