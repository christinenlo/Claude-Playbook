🔍 Web search & Research

Claude can look things up online and pull from a web page — or do a deeper, multi-source dig.

Web search

Claude can look things up on the web, and whenever it does, its answer comes with clickable citations so you can check the source. In Cowork, it searches on its own whenever a task calls for it — you don’t have to switch anything on. You can also paste a link and Claude will read that page to summarize it or pull details from it.

(In regular Chat you may see a web-search toggle by the message box. On a Team/Enterprise workspace, an owner may need to switch web search on first.)

Research (a deeper dig)

For a bigger question that needs several sources, there’s Research — Claude runs a series of linked searches over a couple of minutes and comes back with a written, cited report. It’s good for market or regulatory digging where one quick search isn’t enough. If you want the thorough version and Claude doesn’t do it on its own, just ask — “please do some deeper research on this” or “use the research tool.” It uses more of your usage than a normal answer.

Which one?Quick fact or a specific page → a normal answer with web search. Thorough, many-source answer → ask for Research. Your own accounts and documents → keep those in a project or use the Microsoft connectors.
