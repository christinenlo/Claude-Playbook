Web search & Research

Quick look-ups, or a deeper multi-source dig.

Web search

In Cowork, Claude searches the web on its own when a task needs it, and its answers come with clickable citations. You can also paste a link for it to read and summarize.

Research (a deeper dig)

For a bigger question, ask for Research — “please do some deeper research on this.” Claude runs several searches over a couple of minutes and returns a cited report. Good for market or regulatory questions; it uses more of your usage.

Which one?Quick fact or one page → normal answer. Thorough, many sources → Research. Your own accounts and documents → a project or the Microsoft connectors.
