2Download the Claude Chrome Extension

Do this in your NEW profileMake sure you're in the Claude profile from Step 1 before installing.

Install link (copy & paste into Chrome): https://chromewebstore.google.com/detail/claude/fcoeoabgfenejglbffodgkkbkcdhcgfn

Or search "claude extension chrome" in Google.

  1. Open the link in Chrome and click Add to Chrome.
  2. In the pop-up, click Add extension to confirm.
  3. When prompted, click Authorize to connect it to your Claude account.
  4. Click the Extensions icon (puzzle piece, top-right)…
  5. …then pin Claude so it stays in your toolbar.
  6. Click the Claude icon → the side chat opens → press Log in, then log in with your email.
