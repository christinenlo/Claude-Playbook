2Set Up the Claude Chrome Extension

Do this in your NEW profileMake sure you're in the Claude profile from Step 1 before installing.

The Claude extension is already installed on your computer — you just need to pin it and log in.

  1. Click the Extensions icon (puzzle piece, top-right)…
  2. …then pin Claude so it stays in your toolbar.
  3. Click the Claude icon → the side chat opens → press Log in, then log in with your email.
