Org Skill · Carrier Submissions

Every carrier skill works the same way.Arden, LIO, PMA, CA FAIR Plan, Honeycomb — they all follow the identical steps below. Just pick the matching skill from the Org Skills Directory and attach the SOV. The only thing that changes is which portal opens and which skill name you type.
  1. Before running, confirm you're on your new Chrome profile (check via the profile icon top-right), on Sonnet, with Extended Thinking OFF.
  2. In Claude Desktop, type / to bring up the skill menu and pick the skill you want. (You can ignore the grey helper text.)
  3. Use + → Add files or photos to attach your SOV, then press Enter or click Let's go. Claude runs in the background and pings you when it needs you or finishes.
  4. Claude opens the correct carrier site and asks you to log in. Log in manually — never save passwords or give Claude your credentials. Then tell it to continue (e.g. "I'm logged in, please continue").
  5. When prompted for browser permission, choose "All for this website" from the dropdown so it can keep working uninterrupted.
  6. Review before you submit. When Claude finishes filling the portal, check every field against the SOV yourself. Claude does the entry — you own the accuracy. Don't hit submit until it looks right.
