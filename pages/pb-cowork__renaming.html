Renaming & quick actions

Give chats clear names so they’re easy to find. The same menus have a few other handy actions.

Inside an open chat or task

Click the ▾ next to the title, then Rename to edit the name in place.

From the list on the left

Hover a chat and click the … that appears.

ActionWhat it does
PinKeeps it at the top — see Pin your chats.
Add to projectFiles it into a project.
ScheduleRuns it on a repeating schedule.
Turn into skillSaves a task you repeat as a reusable skill.
Archive / DeleteHides it without deleting / removes it for good.
