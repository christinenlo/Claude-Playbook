Org Skill · Training Docs

Generates a polished HOA Insurance training document (PDF) from a call transcript (.vtt) and/or any supporting files you provide.

  1. Run the skill /lo-create-training-docs. Confirm the model is Sonnet.
  2. Upload any source files — PDF, Excel, Word, PowerPoint, .vtt, etc.
  3. Add any extra instructions, then press the orange arrow or Enter to generate the training document.
  4. Once it’s done, Claude generates a PDF of your training document — ready to review, download, or share.
