🛡️ Working safely with client data

A few simple habits that keep client info safe when Claude works with your files and the browser.

Claude can read, edit, and act on your files, your apps, and web pages — which is what makes it so useful. Because you work with client PII, a handful of habits keep everything safe. None of this is complicated.

Approve as it goes

For anything sensitive, keep Claude on “approve each action” rather than letting it run unattended. You’ll see a prompt before it does something, and you can watch what it’s doing. Claude also asks before permanently deleting a file — read those prompts before you click.

Only run on files you trust

A file or email from outside (a vendor template, a loss run, a broker’s message) can occasionally contain hidden text that tries to redirect Claude — for example, telling it to pull data it shouldn’t or change something. Claude is built to resist this and will ask you to confirm anything risky, so read those confirmations carefully. When in doubt, work on a trusted copy and be specific about what you want changed.

⚠️ Don’t point Claude in Chrome at pages with client PIIClaude in Chrome takes a picture of your active tab to see it, so avoid using it on pages showing client personal info or sensitive carrier-account data. Anthropic specifically recommends against using it on regulated data. It’s great for navigating a portal or a submission form — just be mindful of what’s on the screen while it works.

Good habits

  • Share a specific folder, not your whole drive — Claude only ever sees what you give it.
  • Close confidential files you don’t need open while a task runs.
  • Review before it’s final. Claude is fast, but you’re the expert — check anything headed to a carrier or client.
  • Keep dashboards private. If you build a tracker or artifact with client info, don’t share it with a public link.
