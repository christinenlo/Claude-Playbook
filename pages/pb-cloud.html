Claude in the cloud

Run a task in the cloud so it keeps going after you close the app — and pick Claude up on web, desktop, or mobile.

What it is

When you give Claude a task, you can run it in the cloud instead of on your own computer. A cloud task keeps going even when you close the app or shut your laptop — Claude carries on where it left off, and you can check on it from any device.

Choosing where a task runs

Click the screen icon next to the message box and pick:

  • In the cloud Beta — keeps going even when you close the app.
  • On your computer — only runs while your computer is on.

On web, desktop & mobile

Claude Cowork works in the desktop app, on the web (claude.ai), and on mobile (iOS & Android). Your tasks and files sync across devices through your Claude account — so you can start something at your desk, check it from your phone, and get a notification when Claude needs your input.

⚠️ Local-file & browser work still needs the desktop appThe cloud is great for tasks that don’t touch your computer — research, drafting, summarizing. But anything that needs your local files, the browser (Claude in Chrome), or Epic — like SOVs, ACORDs, and portal submissions — still needs the Claude Desktop app open on your computer. Web and mobile can start those tasks, but your desktop has to be running for Claude to reach your files.
