Claude in the cloud

Your work runs on Anthropic’s servers and follows you across devices.

What it is

Cowork runs in the cloud, so a task keeps going after you close the app or shut your laptop. Nothing to switch on — your sessions and files live with your Claude account.

On web, desktop & mobile

Cowork works in the desktop app, on the web (claude.ai) and on mobile, with the same sessions and files on all three. Start a task at your desk, check it or answer Claude from your phone, then carry on at your computer. (Web and mobile are still in beta.)

Scheduled tasks in the cloud

Scheduled tasks run in the cloud too — even when your computer is asleep. A 7 am renewal summary is ready before you sit down. A task that needs your local files or Claude in Chrome needs Require this computer turned on, and then only runs while your computer is awake (see Setting one up).

⚠️ Local files, the browser and Epic still need the desktop appResearch, drafting and anything using connectors (Outlook, SharePoint, Teams) run fine in the cloud. But SOVs, ACORDs and portal submissions reach your files and browser only while Claude Desktop is open on your computer — even if you start them from your phone.
