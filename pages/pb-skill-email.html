Org Skill · Email Tracker

Builds a live Cowork artifact of your Outlook mailbox — what’s come in, which sent emails are still waiting on a reply, and anything you’ve flagged — with one-click draft replies and follow-ups.

Set it up

  1. Check Microsoft 365 is connected. Your name (bottom-left) → Settings → Connectors → Microsoft 365 (Outlook) should say Connected (if not, click Connect and sign in once). While you’re there, open Skills and confirm /lo-email-tracker-artifact is on. The tracker reads your mail through this connector — without it, the skill stops and asks you to connect.
  2. Run the skill. In a new chat, type /lo-email-tracker-artifact and press Enter. No extra details needed.
  3. Answer the one question. Track your own inbox or a shared mailbox (support/board)? For a shared mailbox, have the exact email address ready. Building takes a minute or two.

The three tabs

Inbox — everything that’s come in. Unread messages are bold with a blue dot; click a card to read the full message.

Sent — what you’ve sent and whether it got a reply. The tiles up top total each status, and every email is labeled Replied, Awaiting reply, or Needs follow-up (no reply after 5 business days). Filter chips let you narrow to any of those.

Flagged — anything you’ve flagged with the flag icon. Flags save to Outlook as a category, so they stick around and show up in Outlook too.

Drafting a reply or follow-up

On any card, click Generate reply or Generate follow-up and Claude writes a draft.

Review and edit it, then choose Create draft in Outlook (saved to your Drafts, kept threaded to the original) or Send now (sends immediately as a new email). Either way, nothing goes out until you press a button.

Good to know

  • It refreshes when you return to the tab after ~5 minutes away (not on a timer).
  • Shows the last 14 days first, then fills to 30 — pick 60 days to fetch more.
  • Shared mailboxes are read-only — tracking works, but flagging, drafts, and sending are off.
  • Run the skill in your own chat so it connects to your own mailbox — don’t pass a finished tracker around.
