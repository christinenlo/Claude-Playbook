Q&A

A running list — we’ll keep adding as questions come up.

Setup & login

Which Chrome profile should I be in?

Your Claude Profile (the different color) for anything with Claude or carrier portals. Everyday browsing stays in your personal profile.

Do I sign the new profile into Google?

No — choose Stay signed out, and don’t save passwords to it.

What do I log in with?

Your own account, in both the Chrome extension and Claude Desktop — unless you’ve been assigned to Athena. Need a verification code? Ping @Christine Nguyen.

Chrome or Edge?

Chrome only. This setup doesn’t work on Edge.

Which model and settings should I use?

Sonnet, with Effort on High (the default) and Thinking off. Don’t leave Effort on Max — it uses 2.5× or more of your usage. See Which model to use.

Everyday use

Do I have to keep Claude open? Can I use it on my phone?

Cowork runs in the cloud, so tasks keep going after you close the app, and the same sessions are on desktop, web and mobile. The exception: anything using your local files or the browser (SOVs, ACORDs, portal submissions) needs Claude Desktop open on your computer.

Does Claude need internet?

Yes. If your connection drops, Claude pauses — once you’re back, say “please continue.”

Can I pause and come back to a task later?

Yes. If Claude is waiting on you, nothing times out, and your chats are always saved — reopen it from Recents and say “please continue.”

Can Claude make mistakes?

Yes — review anything headed to a carrier or client, and tell Claude what to fix in plain English. See When to use skills.

Should I use the Fable model?

No — it’s much slower and uses far more of your usage, with no benefit for our work. Use Sonnet, or Opus for hard tasks and building skills.

I need to attach a lot of files — what if I hit the limit?

Attach them in batches and say “don’t do anything yet, I’m still uploading.” Then give the go-ahead. See Uploading files.

Does a scheduled task still run if my computer is off?

Yes — they run in the cloud by default. Only a task with Require this computer turned on needs your computer awake. See Setting one up.

My scheduled task says “Permissions needed.” What do I do?

It’s waiting for your OK — click Review and approve it. To stop that on a routine task, edit it and set Permissions to Automatically approve (recommended). See Managing your tasks.

Can Claude look things up online?

Yes, with clickable citations — or paste a link for it to read. For a bigger question, ask for Research. See Web search & Research.

Can Claude edit my Excel file, or make a Word doc or PDF?

Yes. The Claude for Excel add-in works inside your workbook, and Claude can create Excel, Word, PowerPoint or PDF files for you to download. See Claude in Excel & Office.

Can I hide sidebar items I don’t use?

Yes — right-click More → Edit sidebar…, untick what you don’t want, and click Done. See Claude sidebar.

Security & passwords

Why a separate Chrome profile?

It keeps secure work apart from everyday browsing, so a problem in one can’t reach your portal sessions in the other.

Why never save passwords in the new profile?

It stays credential-free for security. Copy a password from your personal profile only when you need it, and click Never if Chrome offers to save it.

What are the recommended security settings?

Enhanced protection on, third-party cookies blocked, and optionally delete site data when Chrome closes. These are already set up in your Claude profile.

Do I need to turn off autofill?

It’s optional but recommended — see Turn off autofill. Either way, never save personal passwords in this profile.

How do I keep a chat private, or stop it being saved?

Use incognito — the ghost icon (top-right) or Ctrl+Shift+I. It isn’t saved, so don’t use it for work you need, and your admin can still see it. See Incognito mode.

Running skills

How do I start a skill?

Type / in Claude Desktop and pick one. The Org Skills Directory lists them all.

What file does a skill need?

Most carrier skills need the SOV (Excel); the directory says what each needs. Attach it with + → Add files or photos.

Does Claude log into the carrier site for me?

No — always log in yourself and never give Claude your password. Then say “I’m logged in, please continue.”

Claude keeps asking for browser permission.

Pick “All for this website” from the dropdown on the prompt.

Can I do other things while a skill runs?

Yes — skills run in the background, each in its own Chrome tab, and ping you when needed. You can run several at once.

How do I make my own skill?

On Opus, start with /skill-creator and walk Claude through the task like training a new hire — or open a finished chat’s ▾ menu → Turn into skill. See How to create a skill.

How do I get my skill added for everyone?

Ping @Christine Nguyen or @Joon Jung — we’ll review it with you and add it to the Org Skills Directory.

What’s the Email Tracker?

A skill (/lo-email-tracker-artifact) that builds a tracker of your Outlook — new mail, sent emails awaiting a reply, flagged items — with one-click drafts. Needs Microsoft 365 connected. Trackers built before August 19 can’t be edited; run the skill again. See Email Tracker.

Can Claude build a new-business proposal from the quotes?

Yes — /lo-build-formalizedcomparison-proposal fills the NB - CONDO proposal from your quotes and can merge in a comparison sheet. Condos only for now. See New Business Proposal.

Sharing, projects & usage

Can I share a chat with a coworker?

Yes — Share (top-right) → Shared → create the link. It shares messages up to that moment, without attached files. Cowork tasks can’t be shared. See Sharing chats & tasks.

If I share a project, can people see my chats in it?

No. They get its instructions and context files; everyone’s chats stay private. See Folders & sharing.

Can I share a project with everyone at the company?

No — company-wide projects are turned off (that’s why the Labarre/Oksnee option is greyed out). Share with specific people instead: open it → Share. See Folders & sharing.

Can I give Claude a folder on my computer inside a project?

Yes — use the project’s Folder panel. It’s only yours, and Claude reaches it only while the desktop app is open. For team files, use Context. See Folders & sharing.

How do I share an artifact, and who can see it?

Click Share on it. Invite people (Viewer, Commenter or Editor) or open it to anyone at Labarre/Oksnee with the link. It can’t be opened outside the company. For client details, use Only people invited. Each viewer sees their own connected data. See Sharing & comments.

How do I rename a chat or task?

Click the ▾ next to its title → Rename, or hover it in the sidebar → … → Rename. See Renaming & quick actions.

How do I pin a project to the sidebar?

Click the pin icon at the top-right of the project, or use its ⋮ menu. See Pin your projects.

How do I check my usage? What’s the 5-hour limit?

Your name (bottom-left) → Settings → Usage. The current session is a rolling ~5-hour window that resets on its own; there’s also a weekly limit. See Token usage.

Is what I share with Claude private?

Claude only sees files and folders you share with it. Your chats and tasks stay private unless you share a chat or an artifact. The Microsoft connectors only take actions (like sending email) that your admin has switched on, and follow your approval settings. Never paste passwords into a chat.

Is our client data used to train Claude?

No — on our plan, your chats and files aren’t used for training (incognito included). That’s why account work belongs on your work account. See Working safely with client data.

Errors & when Claude is down

I got an error message on startup.

Screenshot it and ping @Christine Nguyen. Common ones are listed in Troubleshooting.

Claude’s workspace won’t start, or I see a “VM” error.

Try these in order: 1. Close Claude fully and reopen it. 2. Restart your computer. 3. Shut down completely, wait a few minutes, and turn it back on. If Claude itself is down (check status.claude.com), a restart won’t help — wait 15–30 minutes.

Claude says it can’t connect to Chrome.

Open Chrome on your Claude profile first, and check the extension is installed and logged in (Step 2). Then tell Claude to try again. See Claude in Chrome.

What should I do while Claude is down?

Hold off on submissions until status is green. Don’t reinstall — outages aren’t fixed on your end.
