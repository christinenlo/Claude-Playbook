Org Skill · Create SOV

Fills the HOA Statement of Values on the company template from whatever you have — dec pages, a prior SOV, loss runs, broker emails, coverage summaries, SB1525s.

  1. On Sonnet, attach your files with +, type /lo-create-nb-sov, and send.
  2. Review the summary. Before writing anything, Claude shows every value it found and where each came from.
  3. Answer its questions. It asks rather than guesses. Anything it can’t confirm is left blank and highlighted yellow for you to fill in.
  4. Move the file — click Show in folder, then copy it to the folder you want before opening it.
“File path too long”?Excel sometimes can’t open it from where Claude saved it. Copy it to your Desktop or Downloads first, then open it.
