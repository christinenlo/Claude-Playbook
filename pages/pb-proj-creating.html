Creating a project

Click Projects in the sidebar, then New project (top-right). The Projects screen has three tabs:

  • Your projects — ones you created.
  • Organization — company-wide projects. These are turned off for us, so it’ll be empty.
  • Shared with you — projects a teammate invited you to.

In Create a project:

  • Name it and describe what you’re trying to achieve.
  • Use a folder (optional) — a folder on your computer for Claude to work in. You can add one later too (see Folders & sharing).
  • Visibility — leave it on Private. (The Labarre/Oksnee option is greyed out.) You can still share it with specific people later.

Click Create project. To move a chat into (or out of) a project, use its menu → Add to project (see Renaming & quick actions).
