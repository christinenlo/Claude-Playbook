Org Skill · Comparison Sheet

Builds a one-page LaBarre Oksnee comparison sheet (PDF) — expiring vs. renewal, or several options side by side for the board.

  1. On Sonnet, type /lo-insurance-comparison-sheet and attach the expiring dec pages, the SB1525 disclosure and the renewal / option proposals. Send.
  2. You get a one-page PDF: coverages by section, one column per option, annualized premium at the bottom.

Check the figures against the proposals before it goes to a board or client.
