Org Skill · Comparison Sheet

Builds a one-page LaBarre Oksnee coverage comparison sheet (PDF) that puts the options side by side — expiring vs. renewal, or a couple of options for the board to choose from.

  1. In Cowork (on Sonnet), type /lo-insurance-comparison-sheet and attach the inputs — the expiring dec pages, the SB1525 disclosure, and the renewal / option proposals — plus any other supporting files you want to include. Then press the orange arrow or Enter.
  2. Claude builds a one-page comparison sheet (PDF) — coverages grouped by section, each option in its own column, with the annualized premium at the bottom.
Review before it goes outCheck the figures against the proposals before sharing with a board or client — the sheet notes that values are as presented and aren't a substitute for the full policy terms.
